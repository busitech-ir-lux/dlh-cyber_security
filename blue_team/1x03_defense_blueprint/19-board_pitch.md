# Board Pitch

## Current State

After four weeks of assessment, MedDefense has a **High to Critical cybersecurity risk posture**. Its flat network, missing multi-factor authentication, outdated systems, connected backups and lack of centralized monitoring allow one compromised account or device to threaten the entire hospital.

## The Risk

A breach of the EHR could expose 50,000 patient records and create an estimated **$3.025 million in expected annual loss** through investigation, notification, litigation and lost patient trust. Doing nothing also leaves MedDefense exposed to ransomware, operational downtime, regulatory penalties and possible disruption to patient care.

## The Plan

We recommend approving the full **$120,000 security program**. The three highest-priority controls are multi-factor authentication, network segmentation and Wazuh centralized monitoring, with a combined modeled annual risk reduction of approximately **$5.04 million**. The remaining budget funds endpoint detection, immutable offsite backups and a managed firewall for Westside Clinic. Together, these controls reduce unauthorized access, stop attackers from moving freely through the network, improve detection and protect MedDefense’s ability to recover.

## The Return

The modeled return is approximately **$42 in expected annual loss avoided for every $1 invested**. Although these estimates include overlapping scenarios and are not guaranteed savings, the analysis clearly shows that the financial and operational cost of inaction is much greater than the proposed investment.
