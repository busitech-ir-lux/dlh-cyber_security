## Data Risk Summary

MedDefense’s biggest data protection weakness is **Restricted patient data in transit and in use across the flat internal network**. EHR data, medical images, patient-monitoring data and medication information all move through a network where workstations, servers and medical devices can reach one another without proper segmentation. This risk becomes more serious because unattended EHR sessions, shared accounts, unmanaged iPads, old medical-device software and weak monitoring make unauthorized access difficult to prevent and detect.
