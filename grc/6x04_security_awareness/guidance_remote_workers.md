# Security Quick Reference: Remote Workers

[MedDefense branding placeholder]  
Version 1.0 · 2026-11-10 · Draft for approval

## Your Biggest Threats

- Home network exposure: work starts on a home router with unchanged/default settings. Watch for default administrator credentials and outdated router software.
- Fake remote-access compliance: an email asks for MFA enrollment or VPN verification. Watch for an unexpected login link or prompt you did not start.
- Document and screen leakage: personal cloud, shared devices or public seating exposes work. Watch for personal account upload prompts and people able to view your display.

## What to Do Right Now

- This week, change default router administrator credentials, install available router updates and use WPA2/WPA3 with a strong unique WiFi passphrase; ask IT if unsure.
- Connect the approved VPN on your managed work device before opening any work system; open known portal bookmarks and verify unexpected MFA tasks with IT.
- Keep work on the managed work machine and approved storage; keep household users off it, lock it when away and shield the screen in public.

## When Something Looks Wrong

Report it to Security: select the suspicious email in Outlook and use the phishing report button; for a call, access concern or unavailable button, call **ext. 4-SECURITY**. Report even after clicking. You do not need to prove an attack.

## What Not to Do

- Do not open work systems before establishing the approved VPN connection.
- Do not use personal email or personal cloud storage for work documents.
- Do not let family use the work device or leave work visible in shared/public spaces.

## Contact

Security: **ext. 4-SECURITY · security@meddefense.org**. Outlook: select the message, then choose the organization's phishing report button. Exact label and ribbon/menu location must be confirmed by IT and inserted before publication; the supplied material does not specify them.
