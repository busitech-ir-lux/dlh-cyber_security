Positive delta means increase, not automatically improvement: clicks, repeats, latency and excess burden should fall; reports, verification and completion should rise. ### Status Rules (Proposed)

- Green: validated metric meets the applicable target and workload/safety conditions.
- Yellow: proportion misses its target by ≤5 percentage points; mean latency exceeds its ceiling by ≤5 minutes; confidence improvement misses by ≤5 percentage points of relative improvement; or a required metric is not yet measurable. Unknown baseline established by its planned deadline may be Green for that establishment milestone, but cannot be treated as good performance.
- Red: proportion misses by >5 points, latency exceeds by >5 minutes, confidence improvement misses by >5 points, or approved SOC capacity is exceeded. For strict repeat thresholds, equality is Yellow. For false-positive reports: Green if within capacity and ≤baseline +0.5 pp; Yellow if capacity is met but rate exceeds that ceiling by ≤0.5 additional pp; Red if above that band or capacity exceeded. Higher benign reporting is a workflow review signal, never employee wrongdoing.
- Overall Red: any required behavior metric Red, material privacy/patient-care/welfare incident, or unresolved real-incident pause. Overall Yellow: no Red but any required metric Yellow/N/A, missing required approval or noncomparable data. Overall Green: all due metrics Green, no unresolved harm and sufficient validated data. Do not average colors to conceal a high-impact problem.

Append each quarter: delivered/eligible counts; unique people; segment/template/tier; cohort sampling; clicks after scanner filtering; reports before/after click; observation-window closure; latency distribution and reporter count; assigned/completed training; pair eligibility; SOC adjudication backlog; verification observations; excluded/deferred counts; complaints and pauses; spend; concurrent controls. Aggregate across waves using summed numerators/denominators, not the arithmetic mean of percentages; repeated recipients make pooled intervals descriptive rather than independent-person inference.

## 3. Incident Correlation Methodology

1. Maintain a monthly data series of malicious mail delivered, confirmed credential exposures, confirmed phishing-linked compromises, payment/document incidents, severity, patient-care disruption and response hours/cost where evidenced. Record affected segment and a restricted incident identifier. Separate actual incidents, exercises and benign reports. Confirmed ePHI exfiltration remains “not confirmed” for the baseline incident.
2. Define cases consistently: a confirmed phishing-linked compromise requires SOC/IR evidence connecting social engineering with unauthorized access or action; a suspicious report alone is not a confirmed compromise. Deduplicate campaigns and label blocked versus delivered threats. Maintain both absolute counts and incidents per 1,000 delivered malicious messages where the exposure denominator can be measured reliably; otherwise mark that rate unavailable.
3. Record exact simulation/training dates and role coverage alongside email filtering, IAM/MFA, service-account remediation, EDR/SIEM changes, staffing, attack campaigns and seasonal workload. Keep access to person-level incident linkage restricted under HR/Legal-approved purpose and retention; board analysis is aggregate. Do not give the CISO individual click histories for correlation.
4. Compare pre/post time series over at least several quarters where historical records exist. No such series is supplied beyond one incident: first collect it. Use same-role/same-tier simulation trends, severity and exposure-adjusted real incident rates. If rollout naturally differs across departments, describe matched comparisons with exposure/workload caveats; do not withhold protection to create a control group.
5. Show uncertainty: n/N, sample sizes, Wilson intervals for appropriate proportions, and descriptive timing distributions. Repeated people, changing lure difficulty and campaign clustering limit independence; involve a qualified analyst before making statistical claims. A rise in reported incidents may reflect improved detection, not more compromise. Better gateway blocking may explain fewer incidents before awareness acts.
6. Attribute cautiously: a temporal association is consistent with a contribution, not a causal estimate. If simultaneous controls prevent separation, state “We cannot isolate the awareness program's effect.” Present observed costs, not invented avoided losses. Any ROI scenario must state incident probability, plausible avoided-event range, unit cost source, program cost and sensitivity; label assumptions and include a zero attributable-benefit case. Current materials cannot support an ROI number.

## 4. Formal Improvement and Pause Triggers

Lead initiates review with SOC Manager and HR within five working days of any Red metric, missed day-90 baseline, two consecutive Yellow quarters on the same metric, or evidence of data/segmentation errors affecting the board conclusion. CISO receives the review and approves corrective actions and funding. The five-day response is a proposed service commitment.

A deteriorating three-quarter trend means two consecutive adverse deltas across Q1→Q2→Q3 (or any three adjacent quarters): clicks/repeats/clinical clicks and latency rising; reports/completion/verification/confidence falling. Formal review triggers when the cumulative adverse change is ≥5 percentage points for proportions, ≥5 minutes for latency, or ≥5 percentage points of relative confidence improvement. Review smaller sustained changes as Yellow watch items; do not dismiss a meaningful segment deterioration hidden by the overall rate. Workload over capacity triggers review immediately.

Review cohort sizes/intervals, lure difficulty, sender exposure, automated scans, timing, exclusions, completion access, staff feedback, real attacks and concurrent technical changes. Record root-cause hypotheses, owner, action, due date and the next comparable measurement; retest through a later approved wave after cooldown, never an immediate surprise lure.

Any employee welfare concern can be raised privately with HR or Security. HR may pause the affected exercise or wave immediately; Clinical Operations can halt unsafe clinical activity; Lead/SOC Manager halt sends for a real incident. Stop first and assess scope without publishing the employee's identity. CISO alone authorizes restart after HR/clinical/SOC clearance as applicable. Do not wait for quarterly metrics or a threshold when welfare or safety is at stake.
