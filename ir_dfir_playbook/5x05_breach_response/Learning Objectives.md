## Learning Objectives

**Technical**

- Activate and execute an IR program across a multi-host, multi-phase incident without procedural guidance
    
- Preserve volatile and non-volatile evidence across four affected hosts under active-threat conditions
    
- Analyze memory, disk, proxy, and Sysmon artifacts to reconstruct a 72-hour attack chain
    
- Execute eradication for a Cobalt Strike implant with staged ransomware and credential exposure
    
- Produce a HIPAA four-factor assessment and a board-quality brief under time pressure
    

**Conceptual**

- Synthesis of all prior incident response, forensic, and improvement competencies
    
- Decision-making under incomplete information with documented trade-offs
    
- Professional communication calibrated to audience, severity, and regulatory obligation
    

**Transversal**

- Everything the student produces must stand alone, no prior context, no verbal explanation
    
- Every decision must be traceable to evidence in the artifacts or reasoning in the record
