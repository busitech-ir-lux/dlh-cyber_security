## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for classifying and protecting HealthPlus information according to its sensitivity, legal obligations, and potential impact if disclosed, altered, or lost.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Applicability

This policy applies to:

- all employees
    
- contractors and consultants
    
- healthcare professionals
    
- temporary workers
    
- third-party vendors
    
- business associates
    

### 2.2 Systems and Assets Covered

This policy covers information stored or processed in:

- electronic health record systems
    
- employee and financial systems
    
- research platforms
    
- cloud services
    
- email and collaboration tools
    
- endpoints and mobile devices
    
- removable media
    
- paper records
    

### 2.3 Exclusions

No information type or system is excluded without an approved exception.

## 3. Policy Statements

### 3.1 Classification Principles

Information must be classified by its owner when created, received, or materially changed.

Where information contains multiple classifications, the highest classification applies.

Access must follow least privilege and business need-to-know principles.

### 3.2 Classification Levels

|Level|Description|Examples|
|---|---|---|
|**PUBLIC**|Approved for public disclosure|Website content, marketing materials, published reports|
|**INTERNAL**|Intended only for normal internal business use|Internal memos, procedures, organizational charts|
|**CONFIDENTIAL**|Unauthorized disclosure could cause financial, legal, privacy, or reputational harm|Employee PII, financial records, contracts, identifiable research data|
|**RESTRICTED**|Unauthorized disclosure could cause severe harm or regulatory impact|PHI, credentials, authentication secrets, highly sensitive research data|

### 3.3 Handling Requirements Matrix

|Requirement|Public|Internal|Confidential|Restricted|
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
|Classification label required|Yes|Yes|Yes|Yes|
|Encryption at rest|No|Recommended|Yes|Yes|
|Encryption in transit|Yes|Yes|Yes|Yes|
|Access control|Public access after approval|Workforce access|Role-based, need-to-know|Strict role-based access, MFA, logging|
|Approved storage required|Yes|Yes|Yes|Yes|
|External sharing approval|No after publication approval|Manager approval|Data-owner approval|Privacy/Security and data-owner approval|
|Secure disposal|Standard disposal|Approved disposal|Secure shredding/sanitization|Documented secure destruction|

## 4. Labeling

### 4.1 Electronic Information

Electronic files must include the classification in:

- the document header or footer
    
- file metadata where supported
    
- the filename for Confidential and Restricted data
    

Examples:

- `PUBLIC_HealthPlus_Brochure.pdf`
    
- `INTERNAL_Department_Procedure.docx`
    
- `CONFIDENTIAL_Employee_Report.xlsx`
    
- `RESTRICTED_Patient_Record.pdf`
    

### 4.2 Paper Information

Confidential and Restricted paper records must display the classification on each page or on the document cover.

Email containing Confidential or Restricted information must include the classification in the subject line or message banner.

## 5. Storage

### 5.1 Approved Locations

Information must be stored only in approved systems.

|Level|Approved Storage|
|---|---|
|Public|Approved website, public repository, corporate storage|
|Internal|Corporate file shares, approved cloud collaboration platforms|
|Confidential|Access-controlled and encrypted corporate systems|
|Restricted|Approved clinical, security, or regulated systems with encryption, MFA, logging, and backup|

### 5.2 Prohibited Locations

Confidential and Restricted information must not be stored in:

- personal email accounts
    
- personal cloud storage
    
- unapproved applications
    
- unencrypted removable media
    
- unmanaged personal devices
    
- public collaboration links
    
- local folders without approved protection
    

PHI must be stored only in approved systems designed and authorized to process health information.

## 6. Transmission

### 6.1 Email

- Public and Internal information may be sent using corporate email.
    
- Confidential information may be sent only to authorized recipients using approved encryption where external transmission is required.
    
- Restricted information must use approved encrypted email, secure portals, or managed file-transfer services.
    
- PHI must not be sent through personal email, ordinary messaging applications, or unencrypted channels.
    

Recipients must be verified before Confidential or Restricted data is sent.

### 6.2 File Transfer

Approved secure transfer methods include:

- encrypted corporate email
    
- secure patient or employee portals
    
- approved managed file-transfer systems
    
- access-controlled collaboration platforms
    
- approved encrypted removable media when specifically authorized
    

Public links and unrestricted sharing are prohibited for Confidential and Restricted information.

## 7. Access Control

Access must be:

- based on job responsibilities
    
- approved by the data or system owner
    
- limited to the minimum necessary
    
- protected by unique accounts
    
- reviewed periodically
    
- removed when no longer required
    

Access reviews must occur:

- annually for Internal information
    
- every six months for Confidential information
    
- quarterly for Restricted information
    

MFA is required for remote access and all access to Restricted data.

Access to PHI must be limited to authorized workforce members and must follow the minimum necessary principle.

## 8. Disposal

### 8.1 Paper Records

|Level|Disposal Method|
|---|---|
|Public|Normal recycling or waste|
|Internal|Approved recycling or disposal|
|Confidential|Cross-cut shredding or approved secure destruction|
|Restricted|Locked destruction bin and certified shredding|

### 8.2 Electronic Media

Electronic media containing Confidential or Restricted information must be cleared, purged, or destroyed using approved methods appropriate to the media and information sensitivity.

Restricted-media disposal must be documented and include:

- media identifier
    
- sanitization method
    
- date
    
- responsible person
    
- verification or destruction certificate
    

Deleting files or formatting a drive alone is not sufficient for Restricted information.

## 10. Compliance

### 10.1 Monitoring

Compliance will be monitored through:

- access reviews
    
- data-loss prevention alerts
    
- storage and sharing reviews
    
- encryption checks
    
- audit logs
    
- disposal records
    
- privacy and security assessments
    

### 10.2 Reporting

Suspected loss, exposure, misclassification, or unauthorized sharing must be reported immediately to:

`privacysecurity@healthplus.example`

### 10.3 Auditing

This policy and its controls must be audited at least annually and after a significant privacy or security incident.

## 11. Enforcement

### 11.1 Violations

Violations may result in:

- retraining
    
- removal of access
    
- written warning
    
- disciplinary action
    
- termination
    
- contractual or legal action
    

### 11.2 Reporting Violations

Suspected violations must be reported to the Privacy and Security Team or Compliance Office.

## 12. Exceptions

### 12.1 Exception Process

Exceptions require:

1. written business justification
    
2. affected data and systems
    
3. risk and privacy assessment
    
4. compensating controls
    
5. approval from the data owner and Privacy and Security Officer
    

### 12.2 Exception Duration

Exceptions may not exceed 12 months and must be reviewed quarterly.

## 13. Definitions

|Term|Definition|
|---|---|
|PHI|Individually identifiable health information protected by HIPAA|
|PII|Information that identifies or can be linked to an individual|
|Personal Data|Information relating to an identified or identifiable person|
|Data Owner|Person responsible for classification and authorized use of information|
|Encryption|Protection that makes information unreadable without an authorized key|
|Sanitization|Process that makes recovery of data infeasible|

# Employee Quick Reference Guide

## Classify Before You Store or Share

|Level|Common Examples|Key Rule|
|---|---|---|
|**PUBLIC**|Website and approved marketing|Share only after publication approval|
|**INTERNAL**|Memos and internal procedures|Keep within approved company systems|
|**CONFIDENTIAL**|Employee PII and financial data|Encrypt and share only with authorized people|
|**RESTRICTED**|PHI and passwords|Use approved systems, MFA, encryption, and strict access|

## Five Rules to Remember

1. Use the highest classification when a file contains different data types.
    
2. Store data only in approved company systems.
    
3. Never send PHI or sensitive data through personal email or unapproved apps.
    
4. Verify recipients before sending Confidential or Restricted information.
    
5. Report lost, exposed, or incorrectly shared data immediately.
    

## Disposal

- Public: normal approved disposal
    
- Internal: approved recycling or disposal
    
- Confidential: secure shredding or media sanitization
    
- Restricted: documented secure destruction
