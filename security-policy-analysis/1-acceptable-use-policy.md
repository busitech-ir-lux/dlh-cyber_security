## 1. Purpose

This policy defines acceptable use of TechSecure Solutions Inc. systems, devices, networks, email, cloud services, and data. Its purpose is to reduce security risks, protect confidential information, and ensure responsible use of company resources.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, temporary workers, interns, and third parties who use company systems or access company information.

It applies to:

- company computers and mobile devices
    
- personal devices approved for work
    
- corporate email
    
- internet access
    
- VPN
    
- cloud development environments
    
- collaboration tools
    
- company data
    

## 3. Policy Statement

Company technology must be used primarily for authorized business purposes. Limited personal use is allowed only when it does not affect work, create security risks, violate laws, or increase company costs.

Users must not use company resources for illegal, offensive, harmful, or unauthorized activities.

## 4. Internet Usage

Users must:

- use the internet mainly for business purposes
    
- avoid illegal, offensive, or unsafe websites
    
- not bypass security controls or web filters
    
- not download unapproved files or software
    

Limited personal browsing is permitted during breaks if it does not affect productivity or security.

The company may monitor internet activity where permitted by law.

## 5. Email Usage

Corporate email must primarily be used for business communication.

Users must not:

- use work email for excessive personal activity
    
- send confidential information without authorization
    
- open suspicious links or attachments
    
- send offensive, illegal, or misleading messages
    
- automatically forward company email to personal accounts
    

Business emails must be retained according to the company’s data-retention requirements. Moreover, no personal data will be extracted from the corporate email for employees upon leaving.

## 6. Software Usage

Only software approved by the IT Department may be installed or used.

Users must not:

- install software without authorization
    
- use pirated or unlicensed software
    
- disable antivirus or security tools
    
- download software from untrusted sources
    

Software requests must be submitted to the IT Service Desk for review and installation. Otherwise, strict system usage and privileges will be applied if bypassing these rules is seen.

## 7. Social Media

Users must not post:

- confidential project information
    
- source code
    
- client information
    
- internal documents
    
- security details
    
- unreleased products or services
    

Employees must not claim to officially represent the company unless authorized. Personal opinions must be clearly identified as personal and must not damage the company, its clients, or employees.

## 8. BYOD

Personal devices may connect to company systems only after approval and registration with IT.

Approved devices must:

- use a screen lock
    
- use encryption
    
- run supported software
    
- install security updates
    
- use approved antivirus or security tools
    
- connect through the company VPN when required
    
- allow company security controls for business data
    

The company may remove company data from a lost, stolen, compromised, or retired personal device. Unapproved personal devices must not connect to the corporate network.

## 9. Data Handling

Users must:

- store company data only in approved locations
    
- share data only with authorized people
    
- use approved collaboration and file-sharing tools
    
- protect confidential data with appropriate access controls
    
- avoid storing company data on personal email, USB devices, or personal cloud storage
    
- securely delete or destroy data when no longer required
    

Confidential information must not be copied, transferred, or disclosed without authorization.

## 10. Roles and Responsibilities

### Users

Users must follow this policy, protect company systems and data, and report suspicious activity.

### Managers

Managers must ensure their teams understand and follow the policy.

### IT Department

IT must approve software, manage devices, maintain security controls, and provide technical support.

### Information Security Department

The Security Department must monitor compliance, investigate violations, review exceptions, and maintain this policy.

## 11. Reporting

Users must immediately report:

- suspicious emails
    
- unauthorized software
    
- lost or stolen devices
    
- accidental data sharing
    
- unusual system activity
    
- possible policy violations
    

Reports must be sent to the IT Service Desk or `security@techsecure.example`.

## 12. Monitoring

The company may monitor company systems, email, internet activity, devices, and network connections for security, legal, operational, and compliance purposes.

Users should not expect complete privacy when using company systems.

## 13. Enforcement and Consequences

Violations may result in:

- removal of system access
    
- required security training
    
- disciplinary action
    
- termination of employment or contract
    
- legal action where appropriate
    

The company may immediately block devices, accounts, software, or network access that creates a security risk.

## 14. Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing and approved by the Information Security Manager and the relevant business owner.

Each exception must include:

- business reason
    
- affected system or user
    
- security risks
    
- compensating controls
    
- expiration date
    

## 15. Review

This policy will be reviewed at least annually and after major security incidents, technology changes, legal changes, or business changes.
